The Rope Ritual

Four steps. Done three nights a week.

Consistency is the whole secret. Here is how our customers use Rope Oil to grow it long and strong.

01 / 04
01

Part and apply

Section clean hair and use the nozzle to drop Rope Oil straight onto the scalp, focusing on edges, crown and thin spots.

Shake first so the herbs wake up
02

Massage it in

Two to three minutes of slow circles with your fingertips. It spreads the oil and gets blood flowing to the follicle.

Wash day: Rope Argan shampoo and conditioner
03

Seal with butter

Smooth a little Secret Hair Growth Butter along the lengths to lock in moisture and stop the breakage that keeps hair short.

A pea sized amount goes far
04

Protect and repeat

Braid, twist or wrap overnight, then repeat three times a week. Take a photo on day one so you can see the change.
